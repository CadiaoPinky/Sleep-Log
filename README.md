App Title: Sleep Log 
Description: A simple sleep log for students. 
Record when you went to bed and when you woke up, and see your average sleep. 

Features: 
*Log your sleep:* choose your bedtime and wake time (date and time).
*Duration preview:* see how long you slept before saving.
*Average sleep:* shows the average time you slept across all saved nights.
*Past logs:* every saved night is listed, with the newest first.
*Delete confirmation:* a message asks "Are you sure you want to delete this?" before a log is removed.
*Form resets after saving:* the bedtime and wake time fields clear for the next night, while your log history stays.
*Saved on your device:* your logs are stored on your device, so no internet or account is needed to see them again.
*Light and dark themes:* switch between a light blue theme and a dark purple theme with one button.
*Installable and works offline:* add it to your phone's home screen and use it like a mobile app, even without internet.

How to Install and Run:
1. Open the live app link in your phone's browser:
   [cadiaopinky.github.io/Sleep-Log/](cadiaopinky.github.io/Sleep-Log/)
2. Android (Chrome): tap the menu (three dots), scroll, then tap install and create shortcut.
3. iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then tap Add to Home Screen.
4. Open Sleep Log from your home screen. After the first visit, it also works without internet.
